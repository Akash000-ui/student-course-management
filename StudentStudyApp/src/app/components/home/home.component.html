Can I preview a course before enrolling?

Yes. Course preview pages show important details before you decide to sign up or continue.

After signing in, enrolled courses are available from the learner dashboard.

The catalog can include Java, Python, AI, data science, web development, SAP, Tally, and other software courses added by the admin.
